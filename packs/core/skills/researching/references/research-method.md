# Research Method

Search combinations of mechanism, objective, failure signature, remedy,
official code, ablation, limitation, and reproduction. Useful adjacent axes
include controllability, weak conditioning, latent priors, exposure bias,
retrieval, planning, calibration, representation bottlenecks, alignment, causal
leakage, and evaluation validity.

Prefer paper/supplement, official project/code, independent artifact-backed
reproduction, then surveys for vocabulary. For important claims record exact
support, date/revision, and whether direct or inferred.

Candidate records should contain mechanism, assumptions, support/contradiction,
code/data status, local change surface, frozen invariants, cost, minimal test,
and failure signature. Reject candidates that violate information availability,
causality, deployment, or matched-comparison requirements.

Archive only reusable PDFs/Markdown, source snapshots, configs, checkpoint
metadata, or evidence notes. Record URL, retrieval time, revision, license when
relevant, and SHA256. Avoid vendoring large artifacts without need.

Translate a candidate into a mechanism hypothesis, minimal intervention,
matched baseline, discriminating measurements, positive/negative signatures,
and stop/expand criteria. The output brief should contain local facts, cited
findings, comparison, recommendation, risks, and exact user decisions.
